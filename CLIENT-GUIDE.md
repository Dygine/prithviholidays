# Prithvi Holidays — how to update your website

No technical knowledge needed. If you can use email, you can use this.

---

## Getting in

Go to **prithviholidays.com/admin/** and type your passcode.

You stay signed in for 12 hours, or until you close the tab.

---

## The one rule worth knowing

Nothing you change appears on the website straight away.

Your edits are saved as a **draft** that only you can see. The website keeps
showing the old version until you press the blue **Publish** button at the
bottom right.

This is on purpose. It means you can change things, look at them, change your
mind, and nobody ever sees a half-finished page.

The bar at the bottom always tells you where you stand:

- **All changes published** — the website matches what you see
- **Unpublished changes** — you have edits waiting
- **1 photo** — a photograph is waiting to go up too

## Adding a travel package

1. **Packages** in the left menu → **New package**
2. Type the name, choose the destination, press **Create and edit**
3. Work through the tabs along the top:

| Tab | What goes there |
|---|---|
| **Basic** | name, destination, how many days, the description |
| **Media** | the main photograph and extra ones |
| **Pricing** | a price, or leave it as "Enquire for pricing" |
| **Highlights** | the best bits of the trip |
| **Inclusions** | what is and is not included |
| **Itinerary** | what happens each day |
| **Publishing** | switch it on when it is ready |

4. Go to **Publishing** and tick **Published**
5. Press **Publish** at the bottom

Everything saves as you type. There is no save button and you cannot lose
work by clicking away.

**Not ready yet?** Leave *Published* unticked. It stays in your list and
nobody else can see it.

---

## Adding photographs

Anywhere you see a photograph, there is an **Upload** button.

1. Press **Upload**
2. Choose a picture from your computer or phone
3. Wait a moment — large photographs are made smaller automatically
4. Press **Publish** when you are done

You can also drag a picture straight onto the upload box.

**A few things worth knowing:**

- Photographs straight from a phone are fine. They get resized for you.
- Wide pictures look better than tall ones.
- A photo is not on the website until you press **Publish**. Until then it
  shows a small orange **New** label.
- **Browse** lets you re-use a photograph already on the site instead of
  uploading the same one twice.

## Changing the pictures at the top of the home page

Those large sliding photographs are the "hero".

**Home & Settings** → **Home hero** tab.

- **Upload photographs** to add more
- Drag them to change the order — the one marked **First** shows first
- The bin icon on any picture removes it

Press **Publish** when you are happy.

---

## Editing what a page says

**Home & Settings** holds all the standing text on the site.

| Tab | Controls |
|---|---|
| **Business** | phone, email, address, WhatsApp, social links, footer |
| **Home hero** | the big sliding photographs and their words |
| **Home page** | every heading and paragraph on the home page |
| **Statistics** | the counting numbers (years, travellers, and so on) |
| **Other pages** | headings for Destinations, Packages, About, Contact |
| **SEO** | what Google shows when people find you |
| **Enquiries** | where the contact form sends messages |

---

## Checking before you publish

Press **Preview** in the bottom bar. Your website opens in a new tab showing
your draft, exactly as visitors will see it once you publish.

Visitors still see the old version while you look. Close the tab when done.

---

## If you make a mistake

**Before publishing** — press **Discard** in the bottom bar. Everything goes
back to how the live website looks. It asks first.

**After publishing** — change it back and publish again. Takes a minute.

---

## Enquiries

**Enquiries** in the left menu shows messages from the contact form.
Click one to read it, reply by email, and mark it Contacted or Archived.

> **Important:** if your admin shows an orange warning on this page, contact
> form messages are **not reaching you**. They are being kept in the visitor's
> own browser. Ask your developer to set a form endpoint. Until that is done,
> rely on the phone number and WhatsApp button.

## Questions people ask

**Will I break the website?**
No. You cannot change the design or the layout, only the words and pictures.
And nothing goes live until you press Publish.

**Can I work on it over several days?**
Yes. Your draft waits in the same browser on the same computer. Come back and
carry on.

**Can two people edit at once?**
No. Drafts live in one browser, so two people editing at the same time will
overwrite each other. Take turns.

**I closed the tab without publishing.**
Your work is safe. Sign back in and it is where you left it.

**How long until a change appears?**
Under a minute. Refresh the page if you do not see it — press Ctrl+Shift+R
(Cmd+Shift+R on a Mac) to be sure you are not seeing a cached copy.

**I forgot the passcode.**
Only your developer can reset it. It is stored scrambled and cannot be read
back, even by them.
